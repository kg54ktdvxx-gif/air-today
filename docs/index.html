Air Today Support

Help for the Air Today app on iPhone and Apple Watch

Need help or found a problem? Email busiest-21.qualm@icloud.com and say which city you were looking at and what you expected to see. Replies usually come within a few days.

Common questions

Why does it say “Measured at …” on one page and “Estimated for this area” on another?
Air Today shows the nearest monitoring station when it has reported in the last two hours and is within 20 km. Otherwise it shows a current estimate for the area from the Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service (CAMS). The line at the bottom of each page always says which one you're seeing and how fresh it is.

Why is the number different from another app or website?
Apps use different stations, different scales and different update times. A station measures one street corner; the estimate covers a few kilometres. Check which scale is shown under Settings → Air Quality Scale.

How do I switch between the US, European and UK scales?
Settings → Air Quality Scale. The verdict, activity advice and alerts all follow the scale you choose. Tap the big number on the main screen to see what each band means.

I'm not getting alerts.
Check Settings → Alerts in the app, and that notifications for Air Today are allowed in iOS Settings. iOS decides how often apps can check in the background, so alerts can arrive a little late.

How do I add or remove a city?
Tap + on the main screen to add one (up to six). To remove a city, swipe left on it in Settings → Places.

Is the advice medical advice?
No. It's general guidance based on published advice from the US EPA (AirNow), the European Environment Agency and Defra. If you have a health condition, follow your doctor's advice.

Data sources

Station measurements: World Air Quality Index Project and its contributing agencies.
Estimates, forecasts and weather: Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0), using Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service information.
