Privacy Policy

Air Today — Effective October 25, 2026

The short version: Air Today does not collect, store, or share your personal data. Your settings and health answers stay on your devices; your location is only used to ask WAQI for nearby readings.

Overview

Air Today is an ambient air quality app for iPhone and Apple Watch developed by Owen McLoughlin. This privacy policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, and your choices regarding that information.

What Data Is Collected

Air Today does not collect any personal data. The app does not require an account, does not use analytics or tracking frameworks, and does not serve advertisements.

The app may access the following information on your device:

  • Location (optional): If you grant permission, the app uses Apple's Core Location framework on your iPhone (and on your Apple Watch, if you use the Watch app) to determine your current location and show nearby air quality data. You can deny or revoke this permission at any time in Settings. The app works without location access — WAQI then estimates your area from your IP address, and you can search for and save locations manually.
  • Health profile (optional): During setup or in Settings you can say whether you have asthma or a respiratory condition, are sensitive to ozone, or have children at home. These answers tailor the advice the app shows. They are stored only on your device and your paired Apple Watch, and are never sent to WAQI or any other server.
  • Saved locations: You can save up to 6 locations within the app. These are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to any server other than as the location of a WAQI data request.

How Data Is Used

Your location, when provided, is used solely to request air quality readings from the World Air Quality Index (WAQI) API for the area near you. The location is sent directly from your device to the WAQI API as part of the data request — it does not pass through any server owned or operated by the developer.

Saved locations, preferences and your health profile are stored on-device using standard iOS storage. Data is shared between the app, its widgets, Live Activity and Apple Watch app through an App Group container, and between your iPhone and Apple Watch using Apple's WatchConnectivity — directly between your own devices. The coordinates of your last check are kept on your device so widgets and background updates can stay current.

Widgets, complications and Live Activities read data stored on your device. When it is more than an hour old, they request a fresh reading from WAQI in the same way the app does.

Notifications (the daily verdict, air-quality alerts, best-time and spike alerts) are created on your device from data the app has fetched. Air Today does not use a push notification server.

Siri and Shortcuts: when you ask Siri about the air, Air Today answers using the same data as the app. Siri requests are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Third-Party Services

Air Today uses the World Air Quality Index (WAQI) API to retrieve air quality data. When the app fetches air quality information, it sends a network request that includes the coordinates or name of the location you are viewing. This request goes directly from your device to the WAQI service.

The WAQI API is operated by the World Air Quality Index project. Their use of data is governed by their own privacy policy, available at aqicn.org/privacy.

Air Today does not integrate any other third-party services, SDKs, analytics platforms, or advertising networks.

Data Retention

Since no personal data is collected or transmitted to the developer, there is nothing to retain on our end. Data stored locally on your devices (saved locations, preferences, health profile) persists until you change it within the app or uninstall the app.

Data Sharing

Air Today does not share your data with anyone. There is no server-side storage, no user database, and no data broker relationships. Your information stays on your device.

Children's Privacy

Air Today does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). Since the app does not collect personal data from any user, it is safe for use by people of all ages.

Your Choices

  • Location access: You can grant, deny, or revoke location permission at any time via iOS Settings > Air Today > Location.
  • Saved locations: You can remove saved locations within the app at any time.
  • Health profile: You can change or clear your answers in the app's Settings at any time.
  • Notifications: Every alert is off until you turn it on, and each can be turned off in the app's Settings or in iOS Settings.
  • Delete all data: Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the revised version will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date. Because the app does not collect contact information, we cannot notify you directly of changes — we encourage you to review this page periodically.
